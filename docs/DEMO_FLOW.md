# DEMO_FLOW.md

## Live Demo Click-Path
1. **Landing Page:**
   - “Welcome to JobFit! Let’s get started.”
   - Click: “Get Started” or onboarding button.
2. **Onboarding Questionnaire:**
   - Fill in target roles, experience, preferences.
   - Click: “Continue.”
3. **Resume Upload:**
   - Click: “Upload Resume,” select PDF.
   - Wait for instant analysis.
   - Show: Resume score, strengths, weaknesses.
4. **Dashboard:**
   - “Here’s your personalized dashboard.”
   - Show: Score breakdown, industry benchmark, strengths/weaknesses.
5. **Find Jobs Page:**
   - Click: “Find Jobs” in nav.
   - Search or pick a job.
   - Click: “Analyze My Fit.”
   - Show: Skill match % and missing skills popup.
6. **Roadmap Graph:**
   - Click: “Generate Roadmap” or skill gap node.
   - Show: Personalized upskilling plan.
7. **Daily Plan / Streak:**
   - Click: “Start Plan” or “Track Streak.”
   - Show: Daily tasks, streak counter.
