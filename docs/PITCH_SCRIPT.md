## The Solution & Demo (0:30-2:00)
Let’s jump right in. Imagine you’re looking for a job:
- First, you land on our JobFit homepage and fill out a quick onboarding questionnaire.
- Next, you upload your resume as a PDF. Instantly, our platform analyzes your skills and shows you a dashboard with your resume score, strengths, and weaknesses.
- Now, head to the Find Jobs page—just like JobStreet, but smarter. Pick any job, and click “Analyze My Fit.” You’ll see exactly which skills you match, which you’re missing, and your fit percentage.
- Click on the skill gap graph to see a personalized upskilling roadmap. Each node is actionable: click to get daily tasks and curated resources.
- Finally, generate your daily plan and start your upskilling streak—just like Duolingo or LeetCode, but for your career.
